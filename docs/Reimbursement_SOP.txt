PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs all expense reimbursements
at NexaCore Technologies, including domestic and international travel, client
entertainment, training expenses, equipment purchases, and miscellaneous
business-related expenditures. The goal is to ensure timely, accurate, and
policy-compliant reimbursement while maintaining financial integrity.

All employees incurring expenses on behalf of NexaCore must follow this SOP.
Non-compliance may result in reimbursement rejection, delayed processing, or
disciplinary action.

Cross-references:
  - Employee Handbook (Employee_Handbook.docx): Section 10 (Travel Overview)
  - IT Setup Guide (IT_Setup_Guide.docx): Section 8 (Equipment Purchase)
  - Leave Policy (Leave_Policy.docx): Section 9 (L&D Reimbursement)

SECTION 1: GENERAL PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

1.1 Guiding Rules
------------------
  a) All expenses must be business-justified and necessary.
  b) Employees must spend as if using their own money.
  c) All expenses over INR 300 must be accompanied by a receipt or invoice.
  d) Expenses must be claimed within 30 calendar days of the date incurred.
     Claims older than 30 days require Finance Head approval and are subject
     to rejection.
  e) Expenses must not be split to avoid approval thresholds.
  f) Expenses incurred for personal benefit are not reimbursable even if
     incurred during a business trip.
  g) All travel must use the most economical option consistent with
     productivity (e.g., economy class for domestic flights under 4 hours).

1.2 Definitions
----------------
  Claimant: The employee submitting the expense claim.
  Cost Centre: The project or department code to which the expense is charged.
  Spend Limit: The maximum permissible amount per expense type per day/trip.
  Approver L1: Reporting Manager.
  Approver L2: Department Head / Delivery VP.
  Finance Reviewer: Finance team member who validates claims before payment.
  Audit: Internal Finance audit of submitted claims (random or scheduled).

SECTION 2: EXPENSE CATEGORIES AND ENTITLEMENTS BY BAND
================================================================================

2.1 Domestic Air Travel
------------------------
  Band 1–2: Economy class only. Booking must be made 7+ days in advance.
  Band 3–4: Economy class only.
  Band 5–6: Economy class standard (business class if flight > 3 hours, with
             prior approval from Delivery VP).
  Band 7–8: Business class permitted for all domestic flights.

Maximum flight cost thresholds (applicable to B1–B4):
  Metro to Metro (e.g., BLR–MUM): INR 8,000 per sector
  Metro to Non-Metro: INR 6,000 per sector
  Non-Metro to Non-Metro: INR 5,000 per sector
  Costs exceeding thresholds require Approver L2 pre-approval.

2.2 Domestic Accommodation
----------------------------
Per-night hotel limits (exclusive of GST):
  Band 1–2: INR 3,000
  Band 3–4: INR 5,000
  Band 5–6: INR 8,000
  Band 7–8: INR 12,000

Company-empaneled hotels: Preferred for all bands. See the Approved Hotel
List on the Finance portal. Use of empaneled hotels does not require prior
approval for amounts within band limits.

Non-empaneled hotels: Require Approver L1 pre-approval.
Airbnb, homestays, or serviced apartments: Require Approver L2 pre-approval.
Personal accommodation (e.g., staying with a relative): INR 500/night
nominal allowance (no receipts needed). Not eligible above B4.

2.3 Domestic Per Diem (Daily Allowance)
-----------------------------------------
Per diem covers meals and local incidentals. It is NOT claimed in addition
to itemised meal receipts; employees choose one method:

  Method A – Per Diem (flat rate, no receipts required):
    Band 1–2: INR 500/day
    Band 3–4: INR 800/day
    Band 5–6: INR 1,200/day
    Band 7–8: INR 1,800/day

  Method B – Actual Reimbursement (receipts required):
    Band 1–2: Up to INR 700/day
    Band 3–4: Up to INR 1,100/day
    Band 5–6: Up to INR 1,600/day
    Band 7–8: Reasonable, no defined cap (subject to Finance review)

Note: Alcohol is not reimbursable under any method or band.

2.4 Local Transport
--------------------
  Within city (taxi/auto/public transport): Actual cost, receipts required
  for amounts over INR 300 per trip.
  Cab/ride-hailing apps (Uber, Ola): Accepted. Receipt from app is sufficient.
  Personal vehicle use: INR 12 per km (for Band 1–4); INR 15 per km (Band 5+).
  Car rental: Requires Approver L1 pre-approval.
  Airport-office transfer: Included in travel entitlement; no separate approval.

2.5 International Travel
-------------------------
International travel entitlements are governed by Section 5. Summary:
  Band 1–4: Economy class only
  Band 5–6: Economy (business class for flights > 8 hours with VP approval)
  Band 7–8: Business class for all international flights

Per diem for international travel is based on destination country and is
updated quarterly on the Finance portal.

2.6 Client Entertainment
-------------------------
  Band 1–4: Up to INR 2,000 per person per event (requires L2 approval)
  Band 5–6: Up to INR 4,000 per person per event
  Band 7–8: Up to INR 7,000 per person per event
  Events with clients: Guest list must be attached. Internal-only events
  are NOT classified as client entertainment.

2.7 Training and Certification
--------------------------------
  Annual L&D budget per employee: INR 25,000
  Individual course/certification limit: INR 15,000 (within annual budget)
  Above INR 15,000: Requires HR and Finance approval
  Reimbursement requires: Course completion certificate or exam result

2.8 Office Supplies and Equipment
-----------------------------------
  Ad-hoc office supplies (stationery, printing): Up to INR 2,000 per month
  with manager approval; receipts required.
  Equipment (keyboards, mice, monitors, etc.): Subject to IT policy and
  pre-approval. Refer to IT_Setup_Guide.docx Section 8.

SECTION 3: PRE-TRAVEL APPROVAL PROCESS
================================================================================

3.1 When Pre-Approval is Required
-----------------------------------
Pre-approval is MANDATORY for:
  - All domestic travel outside the employee's base city
  - All international travel
  - Any single expense expected to exceed INR 10,000
  - Travel during a project critical milestone (last 3 working days of a sprint
    or before a major delivery)

3.2 Travel Request Submission
-------------------------------
Step 1: Employee logs into the NexaCore Finance Portal (finance.nexacore.in).
Step 2: Navigate to Travel > New Travel Request.
Step 3: Fill in:
  - Travel purpose and business justification
  - Destination and travel dates
  - Estimated cost breakdown (flights, accommodation, per diem)
  - Cost Centre code
Step 4: Submit for approval.
Step 5: Approval obtained before any bookings are made.

3.3 Travel Bookings
--------------------
  All flight and hotel bookings must be made through:
    a) The NexaCore Travel Portal (travel.nexacore.in) — preferred and
       recommended.
    b) The designated Travel Desk (traveldesk@nexacore.in) — for complex
       or international itineraries.
    c) Self-booking — permitted only when the travel portal is unavailable.
       Must notify traveldesk@nexacore.in within 24 hours and submit receipts.

  Bookings through personal travel agents or platforms not approved by Finance
  are not reimbursable unless exceptional circumstances are documented.

SECTION 4: DOMESTIC TRAVEL – PROCEDURES AND RULES
================================================================================

4.1 Day Trips
--------------
A day trip is travel where the employee does not stay overnight.
  - No accommodation reimbursement.
  - 50% per diem applies if travel exceeds 4 hours away from base city.
  - Full per diem applies if away for the entire working day (8+ hours).
  - Local transport to/from airport/station is reimbursable.

4.2 Multi-Day Travel
---------------------
  - Accommodation from Check-in Night 1.
  - First day and last day per diem: 75% of full rate.
  - Other days: Full per diem.
  - Weekend stays: Allowed only if travelling back would cost more than the
    additional accommodation, or if a Monday engagement is early morning.
    Approver L1 must approve weekend stays.

4.3 Travel Cancellation
------------------------
If approved travel is cancelled:
  - Cancellation charges are reimbursable if the trip was cancelled for a
    business reason documented in writing.
  - Cancellation due to personal reasons: Charges are borne by the employee.
  - For Travel Portal bookings: Finance team handles cancellation and
    applies policies automatically.
  - For self-booked trips: Employee submits refund received and cancellation
    receipt. Only the non-refunded portion is reimbursable.

4.4 Extended Stays
-------------------
If an employee extends a business trip for personal reasons:
  - The business portion of expenses is reimbursable.
  - The personal extension portion (accommodation, per diem, return flight
    difference) is borne entirely by the employee.
  - If the personal extension actually reduces the company's return flight cost
    (e.g., weekend stay to get a cheaper Monday flight), the company reimburses
    up to the cost of the original flight; the employee pays the difference
    in accommodation.

SECTION 5: INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL – PROCEDURES AND RULES
================================================================================

5.1 Pre-Travel Requirements
----------------------------
In addition to standard travel approval, international travel requires:
  a) Business Visa (if applicable): Supported by HR and Admin team.
  b) Foreign Currency: Obtainable via petty cash advance (see Section 11)
     or using the corporate card (see Section 12).
  c) International SIM or Roaming Plan: Reimbursable up to the plan
     approved by IT. Refer to IT_Setup_Guide.docx, Section 5.
  d) Travel Insurance: NexaCore's corporate travel insurance covers all
     approved international travel. Employees must carry the policy card.
  e) Emergency Contact Registration: Employee must update emergency contact
     in the HR portal before departure.

5.2 International Per Diem Rates
----------------------------------
Per diem rates by region (USD/day equivalent; paid in local currency
or USD as applicable):

  Destination         B1–B4      B5–B6      B7–B8
  North America       $75        $120       $180
  Europe              $80        $130       $190
  Southeast Asia      $50        $80        $120
  Middle East         $60        $100       $150
  Rest of World       $50        $80        $130

These rates include meals, local transport, and incidentals.
Accommodation is separate and reimbursed at actuals (within band limits):
  Band 1–4: Up to $150/night
  Band 5–6: Up to $250/night
  Band 7–8: Actuals (Finance review for amounts exceeding $400/night)

5.3 Currency and Exchange
--------------------------
  - Employees must retain original receipts in foreign currency.
  - Conversion to INR uses the RBI reference rate on the date of the expense.
  - ATM or credit card foreign transaction fees are reimbursable up to 3.5%.
  - Unexpended foreign currency must be returned to Finance within 3 working
    days of return, or within 7 days if exchange is required.

SECTION 6: NON-TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENTS
================================================================================

6.1 Mobile Phone Reimbursement
--------------------------------
All confirmed employees are eligible for monthly mobile reimbursement:
  Band 1–2: INR 500/month
  Band 3–4: INR 750/month
  Band 5–6: INR 1,200/month
  Band 7–8: INR 2,000/month
  Submit: Monthly bill or operator receipt via portal. No carry-forward.

6.2 Internet Reimbursement (for Remote Work)
----------------------------------------------
Employees working from home more than 10 days a month may claim:
  Band 1–4: INR 600/month (broadband bill required)
  Band 5+:  INR 1,000/month

6.3 Relocation Expenses
------------------------
Employees relocated at the company's request are eligible for:
  - Packing and moving costs: Actuals (with invoices from approved mover)
  - Temporary accommodation: Up to 14 days hotel at band limits
  - Miscellaneous: Up to INR 10,000 one-time allowance (no receipts)
  Self-initiated relocation is not reimbursable.

6.4 Medical Claim (Out-of-Pocket)
-----------------------------------
For medical expenses not covered by the Group Mediclaim (e.g., consultation
below deductible, non-covered treatment):
  - Not directly reimbursable through Expense Management.
  - Employee must file with the TPA through the health insurance process.
  - OPD Allowance (if part of CTC): Claim through payroll declaration.

SECTION 7: EXPENSE SUBMISSION PROCESS
================================================================================

7.1 Submission Steps
---------------------
Step 1: Collect all receipts (physical or digital).
Step 2: Log into Finance Portal > Expenses > New Claim.
Step 3: Select expense category and applicable trip (if travel-related).
Step 4: Enter each expense line:
  - Date, category, amount, currency, description, receipt upload.
Step 5: Assign the correct Cost Centre code (obtain from your project manager
        if unknown).
Step 6: Submit the claim before the 25th of each month for processing in
        the current month's payroll.

Claims submitted after the 25th are processed in the following month's payroll.

7.2 Receipt Requirements
-------------------------
  Receipts are REQUIRED for all expenses above INR 300.
  Acceptable receipt formats:
    - Original printed receipts
    - Digital invoices (PDF, JPG, PNG) — minimum 150 DPI
    - Email confirmations from hotels/airlines
  Not acceptable:
    - Photos of screens/monitors showing transaction details
    - Handwritten receipts from unregistered vendors
    - Credit card statements alone (without itemised receipt)

7.3 Submission Deadline
------------------------
  General rule: Submit within 30 days of the expense date.
  End of year: All claims for December must be submitted by December 25.
  International travel: Submit within 15 days of return date (for currency
  reconciliation purposes).

  Late submissions (31–60 days): Require Finance Head approval.
  Very late submissions (61+ days): Not reimbursable. No exceptions.

SECTION 8: APPROVAL WORKFLOW
================================================================================

8.1 Approval Tiers
-------------------
  Claim Amount          Approver L1              Approver L2
  Up to INR 5,000       Reporting Manager        Not Required
  INR 5,001–25,000      Reporting Manager        Department Head
  INR 25,001–1,00,000   Reporting Manager        Delivery VP + Finance Head
  Above INR 1,00,000    Reporting Manager        CFO approval required

8.2 Who Approves Travel Reimbursement?
----------------------------------------
For standard domestic travel reimbursement:
  - Claims up to INR 25,000: Reporting Manager only.
  - Claims above INR 25,000: Reporting Manager + Delivery VP.
  - All approved claims then go through Finance Reviewer before payment.

For international travel reimbursement:
  - All amounts: Reporting Manager + Finance Head.
  - Above INR 1,00,000: Also requires CFO sign-off.

8.3 Finance Review
-------------------
After manager approval, the Finance team reviews all claims for:
  - Policy compliance (correct band, within limits, receipts attached)
  - Correct Cost Centre allocation
  - Currency conversion accuracy (international)
  - Duplicate claim detection
  Finance review SLA: 3 working days.

SECTION 9: PAYMENT PROCESSING
================================================================================

9.1 Payment Timeline
---------------------
Claims submitted and fully approved by the 25th of the month:
  → Paid in the same month's salary (last working day of the month).

Claims submitted and approved after the 25th:
  → Paid in the next month's salary.

Claims requiring additional documentation or approval:
  → Processed within 5 working days of completion of all requirements.

9.2 Payment Method
-------------------
Reimbursements are credited directly to the employee's registered bank account
(same account as salary). No cash reimbursements above INR 2,000.

9.3 Partial Payments
---------------------
If part of a claim is approved and part is rejected:
  - Approved portion is paid in the normal cycle.
  - Rejected portion triggers a rejection notice (see Section 10).
  - Employee may appeal the rejected portion separately.

SECTION 10: REJECTED CLAIMS – PROCESS AND APPEAL
================================================================================

10.1 Reasons for Rejection
----------------------------
Common reasons for rejection include:
  a) Missing or inadequate receipts.
  b) Claim submitted beyond the 30-day deadline without approval.
  c) Amount exceeds band entitlement without prior approval.
  d) Non-business expense submitted (e.g., personal meal, entertainment
     without business purpose stated).
  e) Duplicate claim (same expense submitted twice).
  f) Incorrect or missing Cost Centre.
  g) Non-approved vendor or booking channel.

10.2 Rejection Notification
-----------------------------
The Finance Portal automatically sends a rejection notification to the
employee with:
  - The specific expense lines rejected.
  - The reason code for each rejected line.
  - Instructions for appeal or resubmission.

10.3 What To Do If Your Reimbursement Was Rejected
----------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Review the rejection notice carefully. Identify the reason code.

Step 2: For administrative rejections (missing receipt, wrong Cost Centre,
        data entry error):
  - Correct the error and resubmit within 7 days.
  - Resubmitted claims are re-reviewed within 3 working days.

Step 3: For policy-based rejections (over limit, non-eligible expense):
  - If you believe the rejection was in error:
    a) Gather supporting documentation (approval email, exception note).
    b) Submit an Expense Appeal Form via the Finance Portal within 10 working
       days of the rejection date.
    c) The Finance Head reviews the appeal within 5 working days.
    d) If the appeal is upheld, payment is made in the next cycle.
    e) If the appeal is denied, the Finance Head provides a written explanation.

Step 4: If the Finance Head denies the appeal and you believe the decision is
        still incorrect:
  - Escalate to the CFO via the Finance Portal within 5 working days.
  - The CFO's decision is final.

Step 5: If the rejection involves a potential policy ambiguity or systemic issue:
  - Report to Finance Business Partner and HR concurrently.
  - Finance Business Partner will escalate to the Finance Policy Committee.

10.4 Resubmission Limits
--------------------------
An expense line may be resubmitted a maximum of 2 times. After 2 unsuccessful
submissions, the claim is permanently rejected. No further resubmission or
appeal is permitted.

SECTION 11: ADVANCE AGAINST EXPENSES
================================================================================

11.1 When Advances are Available
----------------------------------
Employees may request a cash advance for:
  - Domestic travel expected to exceed INR 5,000
  - All international travel
  - Events or purchases where pre-payment is required

11.2 Advance Request Process
------------------------------
Step 1: Submit Advance Request via Finance Portal > Advances > New Request.
Step 2: Attach approved Travel Request (mandatory).
Step 3: Approver L1 approves the advance.
Step 4: Finance processes the advance within 3 working days.
Step 5: Advance is credited to the employee's bank account.

11.3 Advance Settlement
-------------------------
  - Domestic advances: Must be settled within 7 days of return.
  - International advances: Must be settled within 10 days of return.
  - Settlement = Submit actual expense claim + return any unspent advance.

Unspent advance not returned within the deadline is deducted from salary.

11.4 Outstanding Advance Policy
---------------------------------
Employees with an unsettled advance may not apply for a new advance. Outstanding
advances are flagged in the Finance Portal. More than 2 outstanding advances
in a year results in advance privileges being suspended for 6 months.

================================================================================

12.1 Eligibility
-----------------
Corporate credit cards are issued to:
  - Band 5 and above (automatically upon confirmation)
  - Band 3–4 employees who travel more than 8 times a year (with approval)

12.2 Usage Rules
-----------------
  - Corporate cards may only be used for pre-approved business expenses.
  - Personal expenses on corporate cards are strictly prohibited and must
    be refunded immediately upon discovery.
  - Monthly statement must be reconciled by the 5th of the following month.
  - Lost or stolen cards must be reported to Finance within 4 hours.
    Contact: +91-80-XXXX-XXXX (24x7 Finance helpdesk).

12.3 Credit Limits
-------------------
  Band 3–4: INR 75,000/month
  Band 5–6: INR 2,00,000/month
  Band 7–8: INR 5,00,000/month

12.4 Card Suspension
---------------------

  - Reconciliation is not submitted for 2 consecutive months.
  - A personal expense is not refunded within 7 days.
  - The employee is placed on a PIP or suspended.

SECTION 13: SPECIAL SCENARIOS AND EDGE CASES
================================================================================

13.1 Travel for Training or Certification
------------------------------------------
  - Travel to attend an approved training counts as business travel.
  - All standard travel rules apply.
  - Training fee is claimed separately under L&D budget.
  - If the training is self-initiated (not assigned by manager), travel is
    reimbursed only if the course is on the approved internal course list.

13.2 Travel Injury or Medical Emergency Abroad
------------------------------------------------
  - NexaCore's corporate travel insurance covers emergency medical treatment.
  - Employees must contact the Travel Insurance Helpline immediately.
  - Out-of-pocket medical expenses abroad (up to $1,000) are reimbursable
    with receipts and a claim form.
  - Above $1,000: Insurance claim process must be followed.

13.3 Client-Paid Expenses
--------------------------
  - If a client covers the employee's travel or entertainment, those costs
    must NOT be claimed from NexaCore.
  - If the employee paid and will be reimbursed by the client, the claim
    must be routed through the client billing team, not through the internal
    Finance portal.

13.4 Team Events and Off-sites
--------------------------------
  - Departmental team events (meals, off-site): Organised by the manager,
    approved by Department Head. Claimed by the organiser.
  - Per-person budget: INR 1,500 for standard team lunch; INR 3,000 for
    off-site day event.
  - Venue booking, catering invoices required.

13.5 Toll, Parking, and Baggage Fees
--------------------------------------
  - Tolls: Reimbursable with ETC statement or receipt.
  - Parking (at office or airport): Reimbursable for business travel only.
  - Checked baggage fees: 1 checked bag reimbursable for trips over 5 days.
    Additional bags require L1 pre-approval.

SECTION 14: AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

14.1 Random Audits
-------------------
Finance conducts random audits of 10% of all submitted claims each month.
Employees selected for audit must provide original receipts within 5 working
days.

14.2 Systematic Fraud Indicators
----------------------------------
The Finance system flags claims for manual review when:
  - The same expense is submitted more than once (same date, amount, vendor).
  - Per diem is claimed for dates when the employee's travel portal shows
    no booking.
  - Claim amounts consistently hit the maximum limit across multiple trips.
  - Mileage claims are submitted for dates when no travel request was approved.

14.3 Consequences of Fraudulent Claims
----------------------------------------
Submitting fraudulent, inflated, or false expense claims is classified as
Major Misconduct under the Employee Handbook (Section 12.2) and may result in:
  - Immediate suspension pending investigation.
  - Recovery of the fraudulent amount.
  - Termination.
  - Criminal proceedings if the amount warrants legal action.

SECTION 15: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q1: Who approves travel reimbursement?
A: For claims up to INR 25,000, only the reporting manager is required. For
   claims above INR 25,000, both the Reporting Manager and the Delivery VP
   must approve. All international travel claims also require the Finance Head.
   The CFO approves claims above INR 1,00,000.

Q2: My reimbursement was rejected. What should I do?
A: Review the rejection reason in the Finance Portal. If it's a documentation
   error, correct and resubmit within 7 days. If you believe the rejection is
   a policy error, submit an Expense Appeal Form within 10 working days. If
   the Finance Head also denies it, escalate to the CFO within 5 working days.

Q3: I forgot to submit my claim within 30 days. Can I still claim it?
A: Claims between 31 and 60 days old require Finance Head approval. Claims
   older than 60 days cannot be reimbursed under any circumstance.

Q4: Can I book my own flight and hotel instead of using the travel portal?
A: Self-booking is allowed only when the travel portal is unavailable.
   You must notify traveldesk@nexacore.in within 24 hours and submit all
   receipts. Bookings through personal travel agents are not reimbursable.

Q5: I upgraded to business class on my own. Will NexaCore reimburse it?
A: No. Only the economy class equivalent (or the amount you were entitled to
   for your band) is reimbursed. If you upgrade at your own expense, you pay
   the difference.

Q6: What happens if I submit a duplicate claim accidentally?
A: The Finance system will automatically flag it. No action is needed from
   your side; the duplicate will be removed. If you realise it before the
   review, cancel it from the portal.

Q7: I used a client's cab. Can I still claim transport?
A: No. Expenses already paid by the client must not be claimed from NexaCore.

Q8: My advance is not settled because the Finance Portal was down. Will
    it affect my salary?
A: If the portal downtime is documented and acknowledged by Finance IT, a
    grace period will be granted. Contact finance@nexacore.in with evidence
    of the portal downtime immediately.
